## Core Product Purpose & Capabilities
The main problem OpusGenAI addresses is that creating high-quality product photography and marketing content traditionally requires expensive equipment, studios, photographers, and complex post-production. OpusGenAI simplifies this through AI:

- **Product Photography:** Studio-grade product visuals generated in seconds.
- **Background Removal & Replacement:** Clean background isolation and contextual background synthesis.
- **Image Cleanup & Enhancement:** Retouching and artifact removal.
- **4× AI Upscaling:** High-resolution neural scaling for print & e-commerce.
- **Smart Uncrop:** Canvas expansion and framing correction.
- **Social Captions & Hashtag Generation:** Automated copy generation for marketing campaigns.
- **Brand Templates:** Reusable styling presets for consistent merchant branding.
- **Studio Lighting & Shadows:** Realistic ray-traced and diffusion shadows and highlights.
- **Multi-Platform Canvas Ratios:**
  - `1:1` — Marketplace & product listings (Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Walmart, Shopee).
  - `9:16` — Vertical reels, stories, TikTok-style short videos.
  - `16:9` — Hero banners and wide advertising visuals.
- **Image-to-Video Generation:** Turn static product photos into video ads with multi-shot marketing campaigns, lifestyle environments, and model scenarios.

## Product Template System & Swapnil's Specific Contribution
Templates define:
1. **Required & Optional Uploads:** (e.g., product photo, model photo, reference image).
2. **AI Generation Parameters:** Scene, visual style, camera movement, lighting, model requirements, shot structure.
3. **User Controls:** Video duration, product positioning, model selection, campaign style.

**User Experience Philosophy:**
> **Upload → Choose → Generate → Review → Export**
> Hide complex prompting and raw model parameters behind intuitive commercial templates.

**Swapnil's Specific Contribution Areas:**
- Product UI development and frontend implementation.
- User input & interactive workflow design.
